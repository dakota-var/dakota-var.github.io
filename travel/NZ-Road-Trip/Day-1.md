# Day 1: Wellington to Hokitika

The trip begins with an early-morning Interislander crossing from Wellington to Picton, followed by a scenic afternoon drive through the Nelson Lakes region, Buller Gorge and down the West Coast to Hokitika.

---

## Morning - Wellington to Picton

Arrive at the Bluebridge ferry terminal at or before 7:00 am to check in. The ferry departs Wellington at 8:15 am and arrives in Picton at 11:45 am.

The crossing through Cook Strait and the Marlborough Sounds is an attraction in itself, so take the opportunity to get outside on deck and enjoy the views. Once the ferry arrives in Picton, disembark and prepare for the afternoon's drive.

---

## ~12:30 pm - Depart Picton

Leave Picton and head south through Marlborough before turning inland towards the Nelson Lakes region. The drive quickly transitions from vineyards and farmland to increasingly mountainous scenery.

---

## ~2:15-2:45 pm - St Arnaud & Lake Rotoiti
###### 131 km today / 131 km total

Stop in St Arnaud for a photo opportunity beside Lake Rotoiti, with the surrounding mountains providing a spectacular backdrop. If time allows, take a short walk along the lakeshore to stretch our legs and enjoy the scenery before continuing towards Murchison. [map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/drUkisJy6XwTzU3S6) | [web](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/nelson-tasman/places/nelson-lakes-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/lake-rotoiti-kerr-bay-walks/)

---

## ~4:15-4:45 - Buller Gorge & Lyell
###### 225 km today / 225 km total

Continue through Murchison and the Buller Gorge, where the road winds through steep forested mountains alongside the Buller River. The gorge is one of the highlights of the drive, so there will be plenty of opportunities to pull over for photos and short breaks.

Stop at the old Lyell mining town to explore this historic area that has now been reclaimed by the forest. [map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nGBUZijwyFYYezqy6) | [web](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/west-coast/places/lyell-area/)

Beyond the gorge, the route reaches the West Coast and continues south towards Punakaiki. The dramatic landscape becomes increasingly coastal, with dense rainforest and rugged hills and the Tasman Sea on the other.

---
