# Day 14: Rotorua $\rightarrow$ Waitomo

This is a nature-focused day travelling from Rotorua to Waitomo, exploring a variety of freshwater ecosystems, native forest, limestone karst and short cave walks. The day builds towards Waitomo's underground landscape, while keeping the walks relatively short ahead of the proper caving tour planned for the following day.

---

## ~7:30 am: depart Rotorua
Leave Rotorua after breakfast and head west along SH5 towards Putāruru.

---

## ~8:15-9:30 am: Te Waihou / Blue Spring, Putāruru
Stop at Te Waihou Blue Spring, entering from Leslie Road for the shorter walk to the spring. Follow the walkway through farmland and native vegetation alongside the exceptionally clear Waihou River.

Allow around an hour to walk towards the Blue Spring, enjoy the scenery and return to the van.

[map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/brknVeZpuEw8eA1y7) | [website](https://www.southwaikato.govt.nz/our-district/sport-and-recreation/parks-and-reserves/te-waihou-walkway-blue-spring)

---
## ~10:15 am - 12:30 pm: Maungatautari - Sanctuary Mountain
Continue to Maungatautari - Sanctuary Mountain, a huge predator-fenced eco-sanctuary protecting native forest and threatened species.

Spend around 2 hours exploring the forest. Individual or guided tours are available.

[map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NZpgSASG7SUMpVBr6) | [website](https://www.sanctuarymountain.co.nz/)
